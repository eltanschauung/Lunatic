Lunatic's chest.rs and chest-physics.rs adapt the skin geometry, default UVs,
and damped motion from Female Gender Mod 5.0.0 for Minecraft 26.3.
Original authors: WildfireRomeo, celeste, and pupnewfster.
Credit to pupnewfster.
Upstream project: https://github.com/FemaleGenderMod/FemaleGenderMod
License declared by the supplied jar: LGPL-3.0. The license text is included
in Female-Gender-Mod-LGPL-3.0.txt. The rest of Lunatic retains its own license.
Lunatic changes: Rust/Horizon integration, character-defined size mapping,
fixed 10% motion settings, size threshold, slim-only rendering and preview.
