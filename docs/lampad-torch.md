# Lampad Torch

One item, `lunatic:lampad_torch`; no rarity/variant system. Values use Luanti's Normal torch: 6 damage, 300 uses, and a shared 0.8-second cooldown with the Purification Rod. Creative mode does not spend durability. Neither attack needs ammunition. Interactable blocks retain their normal right-click handling.

- Left-click: red, white and blue stars at 0, 0.15 and 0.30 seconds. Successive volleys alternate left/right. Stars advance at 25 blocks/second along aim, bending exponentially sideways with k=6 and a maximum forward/side distance of 33 blocks. Hits create a two-block-radius entity damage burst, with original red/white/blue particles and sounds; terrain is not destroyed. The original lifetime-based damage bonus is retained.
- Right-click: a randomly coloured star starts at 28 blocks/second, decays by 1.4/second and falls under 22 blocks/second² gravity. It lasts 0.9–1.3 seconds unless it hits something first. Impact creates the original 11×9 flag plus pole, at 0.44-block spacing, for two seconds. A 16-particle red ring expands with v=3, a=-1 for three seconds.
- The flag's server-owned damage zone lasts three seconds, pulses each target at most once per 0.5 seconds, grows to radius 4.5 and height 3, and deals 6 damage per eligible pulse. Direct flag hits also use the torch's 6 damage. Normal armor, PvP, creative immunity and hurt cooldown rules apply; yin-yang orbs' special cooldown bypass is separate.

The native voxel model samples the original OBJ silhouette at 32×32 with a shallow three-voxel extrusion. Only exposed voxel faces are emitted; three colours share this geometry. Original face UVs and textures are retained. Horizon draws these projectile items facing the camera. The torch's warm held light is a client rendering effect; it does not place light blocks or alter world spawning. Particle and damage timing use Horizon's 20 Hz simulation. Bounding-box overlap uses Horizon's entity collision boxes.

Source: Luanti `mods/mcl_lun_items/init.lua` (`lampad_shoot`, `LampadFlagProjectilePrototype`, `LampadFlagZone`, `lampad_star_projectile`), `models/mcl_lun_star_projectile.obj`, the three `mcl_lun_star_projectile_*.png` textures, `mcl_particles_bonemeal.png`, and `mcl_lun_sounds/sounds/se_option.ogg`. The OBJ reference is retained under `tools/reference/`; run `tools/generate_lampad.py` to regenerate voxel geometry. Existing Lunatic source/asset credits and licensing apply.

Validation: the native build and content-package check pass. An offscreen disposable world verified textured voxel stars, the three-shot volley, and the flag/pole/ring effect. No remote multiplayer session was exercised.
